About Me

Exploring the world with wide eyes and a restless mind.

I'm a curious soul with a restless mind and a soft spot for the overlooked. By day, I wrangle code by night, I chase questions - about the secret lives of trees, the science of boredom, the strange genius of octopuses, and everything in between. "The Curious Root" is where I plant those questions and see what grows.

I believe the world is more fascinating than we give it credit for. A walk around the block, a glance at the moss on a rock, a moment of silence - these small things hold hidden depth if you know where to look. My goal is simple: to dig beneath the surface, connect unlikely dots, and share what I find in clear, playful, and occasionally poetic prose.

Whether you're here to learn something new, spark a conversation, or just pass the time curiously, I'm glad you've wandered into this little patch of internet soil. Pull up a chair. Let's grow something together!
